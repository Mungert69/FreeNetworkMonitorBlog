# Google Search Console page clicks — 3 October 2026 snapshot

This snapshot records the pages with the most Google organic web-search clicks, for comparison in later SEO reviews.

## Source and method

- Search Console property: `sc-domain:readyforquantum.com`.
- Search type: Web.
- Most recent final data available when queried: 3 October 2026.
- Comparison windows: 6 July–3 October 2026 (90 days) and 4 October 2025–3 October 2026 (12 months).
- Blog post URLs with and without a trailing slash were grouped together by slug. Clicks and impressions were summed; CTR is recalculated from those totals. Average position is weighted by impressions.
- These are page-level search results, not a measure of article quality or all site traffic.

## Other high-click pages

Over the 12-month window, the homepage received 54 clicks from 15,109 impressions (0.36% CTR; average position 3.6). The dashboard received 15 clicks from 5,624 impressions (0.27%; position 5.1), the GGUF model selection guide received 15 clicks from 21,567 impressions (0.07%; position 9.7), and the download page received 8 clicks from 13,120 impressions (0.06%; position 3.1).

In the most recent 90 days, the homepage received 14 clicks, the dashboard 11 clicks, and the GGUF guide 3 clicks. The blog listing page had impressions but no clicks in these windows.

## Follow-up observations

- The Nmap vs. Zenmap article has the largest impression count among blog posts but a low CTR, making its title and search snippet good candidates for review.
- The AI Nmap automation guide currently gets the most blog clicks, with a stronger CTR and page-one average position.
- Several posts had both slash and no-slash URL rows in Search Console; this report groups them so their clicks are not split across variants.
